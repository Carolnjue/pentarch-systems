Frequently asked questions

Does it really work without internet?{{icon:chev}}

Yes. Selling, receipts, stock and reports all work offline because the system runs on your computer. Sales are kept safely on the till and upload to your cloud backup as soon as the connection returns.

How much internet does it use?{{icon:chev}}

Very little. Syncing sends small text records, not pictures or video, so an ordinary data bundle or home Wi-Fi is enough. Internet is only needed for backup, sync between devices and branches, and updates.

Is my data safe, and who owns it?{{icon:chev}}

Your data belongs to you. It is stored on your till and backed up to the Pentarch cloud, separated from every other business. You can download a full backup or export everything to CSV at any time.

Is it KRA eTIMS compliant?{{icon:chev}}

Receipts already carry your KRA PIN and the KRA tax code for every item (A to E), and the VAT report groups sales by tax code. eTIMS integration is on the way. We will tell every client when it is available and switch it on for you.

What happens when the free month ends?{{icon:chev}}

Choose a plan and pay by M-Pesa Paybill. Nothing is charged automatically and everything you entered stays. If you do not continue, you can still view and export your records.

Can I run more than one branch?{{icon:chev}}

Yes. You get branch comparison, stock transfers between branches and per-branch prices, all under one account.

Can I use my own computer?{{icon:chev}}

Yes. You need a Windows PC or laptop with a Core i3 processor or better, 4 GB RAM and Google Chrome. USB barcode scanners and 58 mm or 80 mm thermal receipt printers work with it.

What support do I get?{{icon:chev}}

WhatsApp and phone support from our Nairobi team, Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm, on +254 729 455 912. Updates are included.
