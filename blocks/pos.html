Questions

What business owners ask.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Pentarch POS runs on a computer at your premises and keeps selling with no internet. When there is a connection it backs up, sends M-Pesa prompts and lets you check sales from your phone.

Can I use the computer I already have?

Yes. Choose setup-only and we install it on your machine and connect your printer and scanner. We will tell you if a machine is too old to run it well.

What if a cashier makes a mistake?

Mistakes are corrected with a reversal that needs a supervisor PIN and a reason. Nothing is deleted, so the record always adds up.

Can I run several shops?

Yes. Each branch has its own tills and stock, and you see all of them together or one at a time, with transfers between them.

What happens after the free month?

If you continue, you pay the set-up and plan in your quote and keep every record. If you do not, you can export your sales and stock records.
