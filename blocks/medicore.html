a day on medicore

From the delivery van to the evening report

A day on MediCore at a pharmacy counter.

  1. 01

    Receive stock

    Scan the delivery. Batch and expiry fill in from the pack's 2D code and the order is ticked off.

  2. 02

    Sell or dispense

    Scan, take M-Pesa, cash, card or account. The receipt prints and stock drops from the right batch.

  3. 03

    Handle returns

    Refunds go back to the original batch, need a supervisor PIN and keep a reason on record.

  4. 04

    Close the shift

    Count the drawer by note and coin. MediCore shows what should be there and asks about any gap.

  5. 05

    Read the numbers

    Profit, best and slowest sellers, dead stock and each cashier's takings, for any day or month.

What facilities ask before they switch

Do we need internet for MediCore to work?{{icon:chev}}

No. The installed system runs on your own computer and keeps working with no internet. When there is a connection it sends M-Pesa payment prompts, backs up and lets the owner check sales from elsewhere. The free online demo does need internet.

What does the 30-day demo include?{{icon:chev}}

A private online copy of MediCore, set up for your facility type and filled with sample data. If you buy, your demo becomes your live system and we install it at your facility.

Can we use the computers we already have?{{icon:chev}}

Yes. Any Windows computer with 4 GB of RAM or more will run it. We install it on your machines and connect your printer and scanner.

How long does it take to start?{{icon:chev}}

One day on site for most pharmacies and clinics: we install, import your item list and prices, set up staff PINs and train the team. Larger hospitals are planned department by department.

Is our data ours?{{icon:chev}}

Yes. It is stored on your facility computer, with backups you can restore. If a plan lapses, after a 7-day grace period the system becomes read-only: you can view and print everything, but new entries pause until the plan is renewed.

Does it handle SHA and insurance?{{icon:chev}}

MediCore records SHA and insurance numbers on patients and bills and produces claim lists. Direct electronic submission depends on certification and integration approvals; ask us for the current status before you buy.

Can the owner see several branches?{{icon:chev}}

Yes. Add branches of any type under one account. The owner sees all of them together or one at a time, and can move stock between them.
