Health sciences

Built for Kenyan health students.

Twelve health labs for nursing, clinical medicine and other health courses: clinical cases, timed OSCE stations with mark sheets, drug and fluid calculations with worked steps, and exam-style questions.

  • {{icon:check}}Follows Kenyan national guidance, including ETAT+, and WHO recommendations
  • {{icon:check}}Vitals, NEWS2 and triage practice
  • {{icon:check}}Works offline once the labs are saved

For learning only. Students always follow current national and facility protocols and their supervisors.
