Under the Hood: engine cutaways

How cars work, shown with animated 3D models you can orbit, slow down and take apart.

Inline-4Firing order 1-3-4-2
Open the lab{{icon:ne}}

  • Engine room

    Inline-4, V6, flat-6, V8 and V12 cutaways with real crankshaft geometry and firing order, slow-motion X-ray and exploded view.

  • Boost Lab

    Feel turbo lag across five set-ups, from naturally aspirated to electric turbo, and record a 0–100 km/h time for each.

  • Engine builder

    Choose cylinders, bore, stroke, redline and induction. See displacement, a live dyno chart and piston speed.

  • Ear test

    Hear an engine idle, blip and rev to redline, view its waveform and guess the car.

  • Stopping distance

    Pick a car, speed, surface and driver state, then see whether it stops before the crossing.

  • Performance map

    Plot any two metrics for every car, with bubble size for weight and colour for body style.

Play: games built from real specifications

Thirteen games and tools on one page, all driven by the published figures for each car.

  • {{icon:play}}Race simulator

    Any two cars, any conditions. Power, weight, grip, gearbox, drag and the thin air at Nairobi's altitude decide the result, over 0–100 km/h, a quarter mile, 1 km or an 80–120 overtake.

  • {{icon:play}}Hot lap

    Drive any showcase car round the Laqim circuit in 3D and beat your best lap.

  • {{icon:play}}Launch control

    React to five red lights and launch on green. Reaction time and 0–100 time recorded.

  • {{icon:play}}Paint Studio

    Repaint a 3D car in factory colours, change the finish and the light, then save a photo.

  • {{icon:play}}3D Silhouette

    Name a spinning, blacked-out car from its shadow.

  • {{icon:play}}Size Up

    Compare two cars at true scale against a 1.75 m person.
