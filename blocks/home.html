Questions

Before you book a demo.

Do your systems work without internet?

MediCore and Pentarch POS are built offline-first: they run on a computer at your premises and keep working when the line or the power goes, then sync when the connection returns. The Portal, BeautySuite and course systems run online inside your website and work well on mobile data.

Can you set a system up for the way we work?

Yes. Each product is configured to your workflows, roles and branding, and we build extra features where you need them. If an existing tool would serve you better, we will tell you.

Who owns the data?

You do. You can export or back up your records at any time, and we only access your system to support it, with your permission.

How long does it take to start?

Most pharmacies, shops and clinics are running in one day on site. Larger hospitals, schools and colleges are set up in planned stages, department by department.

Can you take over our existing website or system?

Usually, yes. We review it first, tell you whether it is worth keeping, and then improve it, move it to secure hosting or rebuild it.

How does this relate to Pentarch Communications?

Pentarch Systems Solutions is the systems and web development business of Pentarch Communications Ltd. Clients get one team for communication, websites and the systems that run their work.
