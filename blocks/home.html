Before you book a demo

Can you set a system up for the way we work?{{icon:chev}}

Yes. Each product is configured to your workflows, roles and branding, and we build extra features where you need them. If an existing tool would serve you better, we will tell you.

How does pricing work?{{icon:chev}}

A one-off set-up covers configuration, data import and training. After that, a monthly or annual plan covers hosting, support and updates. You receive a written quote after your demo. MediCore has a 30-day online demo and Pentarch POS a free month.

How long does it take to start?{{icon:chev}}

Most pharmacies, shops and clinics are running in one day on site. Larger hospitals, schools and colleges are set up in planned stages, department by department.

Can you take over our existing website or system?{{icon:chev}}

Usually, yes. We review it first, tell you whether it is worth keeping, and then improve it, move it to secure hosting or rebuild it.

How does this relate to Pentarch Communications?{{icon:chev}}

Pentarch Systems Solutions is the systems and software company of the Pentarch family, alongside Pentarch Communications and Pentarch Training Institute. Clients can come to one family for communication, training, websites and the systems that run their work.
