the journey

From application to alumni

A student's path through the Portal at Glamourvine Hair & Beauty College, with the screens each person sees along the way.

Step 01 of 12

Applies online

  • Applicant
  • Office

She applies from the college website and uploads her documents. The application lands in the admissions queue for the office to review and send an offer.

Step 02 of 12

Is admitted

  • Office

Accepting the offer turns the applicant into a student, with an admission number, a class and her own sign-in.

Step 03 of 12

Pays fees

  • Student
  • Office

She sees her balance, invoices and the M-Pesa Pay Bill details. The office sees every payment against her record.

Step 04 of 12

Joins a class

  • Office
  • Student

Classes run by intake. Orientation opens first, then the units of her course unlock in order.

Step 05 of 12

Learns online

  • Trainer
  • Student

Trainers publish modules and lessons. She reads, watches and checks her understanding, with her progress shown as she goes.

Step 06 of 12

Sits CATs and exams

  • Student
  • Trainer

Quizzes, CATs and exams open in the portal, drawing questions from the unit question bank.

Step 07 of 12

Gets her marks

  • Trainer
  • Student

CAT and exam marks are weighted into a final result. Results stay provisional until the school approves them.

Step 08 of 12

Proves her practical skills

  • Student
  • Trainer

She logs each practical with a photo. Her trainer checks the evidence and signs it competent or sends it back.

Step 09 of 12

Graduates

  • Director

The school approves graduands, prints the list and holds the ceremony.

Step 10 of 12

Receives her certificate

  • Graduate

Printed or saved as a PDF, with the official seal, both signatures, a serial number and a QR verification code, plus a full academic transcript.

Step 11 of 12

Is verified by an employer

  • Employer

Anyone can scan the QR code or open the link to confirm the certificate is genuine.

Step 12 of 12

Joins the alumni

  • Graduate
  • Office

Graduates stay on the alumni list for job referrals, salon placements and events.

What schools ask

Do we need a new website?{{icon:chev}}

No. The Portal runs inside a WordPress website. If your current site is WordPress we add it there; if not, we can build or move your site at the same time, with Live Edit inside.

Can it fit a school, not only a college?{{icon:chev}}

Yes. Terms, classes, units and fees are configured for your institution, whether a school, college, TVET or training centre.

How do students sign in?{{icon:chev}}

With the admission number the college issues on activation. New applicants register online.

Can parents or sponsors pay fees?{{icon:chev}}

Yes. Fees can be paid by M-Pesa by anyone, and the payment is matched to the student's record and statement.

Can we bring our existing student records?{{icon:chev}}

Yes. We import students, classes and balances from spreadsheets so you start with a complete record.

Can we use our own certificate design?{{icon:chev}}

Yes. The certificate is designed for your college, with the verification QR code added.
