What it does

Every office in the college, on one record.

Admissions, finance, teaching, the hostel and the students themselves work from the same information, so nobody re-types a name or chases a receipt.

Applications & admissions

Applications come in from your website. Admission numbers and log-ins are issued once the admission fee is confirmed.

Fees & M-Pesa

Fee records, M-Pesa payments, balances, receipts and statements for every student.

Students & classes

Student records, classes by intake, class teachers, timetables, attendance and printable ID cards.

Online learning

Modules and lessons for each unit, with quizzes, CATs and exams drawn from a shared question bank.

Marking & gradebook

Online work is marked automatically, essays queue for the trainer, and the gradebook weights CATs and exams into a final result.

{{icon:layers}}

Practical logbook

Students record practicals with photo evidence, and trainers sign each one as competent or send it back.

Graduation & certificates

Approve graduands, print the list, hold the ceremony and print certificates with serial numbers and an online verification code.

Alumni

Hostel rooms and beds, a store for class materials, and notices to students and staff.

Questions

What schools ask.

Do we need a new website?

No. The Portal runs inside a WordPress website. If your current site is WordPress we add it there; if not, we can build or move your site at the same time.

Can it fit a school, not only a college?

Yes. Terms, classes, units and fees are configured for your institution, whether a school, college, TVET or training centre.

Can parents or sponsors pay fees?

Yes. Fees can be paid by M-Pesa by anyone, and the payment is matched to the student's record and statement.

Can we bring our existing student records?

Yes. We import students, classes and balances from spreadsheets so you start with a complete record.
