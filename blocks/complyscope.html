Questions about ComplyScope

Does ComplyScope file returns for us?{{icon:chev}}

No. It tells you what is due and when, and keeps the record. You or your accountant file on iTax or the county portal, then record the reference.

Which obligations does it cover?{{icon:chev}}

Business registration and annual returns, KRA taxes including VAT, PAYE and instalment tax, SHA, NSSF, NITA and the Housing Levy, ODPC registration, DOSH workplace rules, county permits and sector licences.

We have branches in several counties. Does that work?{{icon:chev}}

Yes. Add each location with its county; each one gets its own permit and renewal dates.

Can our accountant use it with us?{{icon:chev}}

Yes. Invite them as an editor to record filings, or as a viewer to see everything without changing it.

Is our data private?{{icon:chev}}

Each business is visible only to the people its owner invites. Documents stay private to the team and are listed in the dossier.
