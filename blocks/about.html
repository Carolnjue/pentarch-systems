who we are

Software built and supported in Nairobi

Pentarch Systems Solutions is the systems and software company of the Pentarch family, alongside Pentarch Communications, the Nairobi studio known for communication strategy, design and campaigns. We grew out of building websites and portals for Pentarch clients, and now build and support our own products.

Clients get one team that understands both the message and the machinery: what an organisation needs to say, and the systems it needs to run its day.
