Who we are

The systems business of the Pentarch family.

Pentarch Systems Solutions is a business of Pentarch Communications Ltd, the Nairobi studio known for communication strategy, design and campaigns. We grew out of building websites and portals for Pentarch clients, and now build and support our own products.

Clients get one team that understands both the message and the machinery: what an organisation needs to say, and the systems it needs to run its day.
