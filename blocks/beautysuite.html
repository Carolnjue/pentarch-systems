Questions about BeautySuite

Which devices does it run on?{{icon:chev}}

The desk computer, a tablet at the stations and phones. It runs in the browser.

Can clients book themselves?{{icon:chev}}

Yes. Clients send booking requests from your salon website, or book a stylist from her own page. Reception confirms them on the desk.

Can it handle a training salon?{{icon:chev}}

Yes. It was built for a salon that is also a training floor for a beauty college, and runs alongside the Pentarch Portal.

Can we take deposits for bridal bookings?{{icon:chev}}

Yes. The quote and deposit fill in from your price list when the booking is made, and the balance is settled at checkout.
