How it works

Three steps. No training manual.

01

Open the page

Sign in and visit the page you want to change. Click "Edit page" at the bottom of the screen.

02

Click and change

Click any text to type, any picture to choose another from the Media Library, any button to change its link.

03

Save

Press Save. The page updates for visitors and the old version is kept in revisions.

{{icon:shield}}

Safe by design

Editors change content, not the layout, so the design stays intact however often the page is updated.

{{icon:layers}}

Header and footer too
